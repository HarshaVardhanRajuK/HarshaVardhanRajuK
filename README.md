I build full-stack systems that handle real users, real scale, and real-world complexity — and I care about the product outcomes as much as the code behind them.

Currently a core engineer at **Bookingjini**, owning end-to-end development of **Hotelkite** — an all-in-one hotel management platform serving 50+ properties. I've shipped three production systems solo: a hotel ecosystem, a government booking platform, and a SaaS product I also helped take from codebase to business plan.

Self-taught. Transitioned from Civil Engineering. I learn by building things that actually work at scale.

## What I've Built

**Hotelkite** — Hotel Management Platform
> PMS · Channel Manager · Booking Engine · AI Pricing · Multi-tenant architecture
> Real-time OTA sync to Booking.com, Expedia, Go-MMT, Agoda via BullMQ job queues. Zero booking loss under high concurrency.

**Banquet Management System** — [Haryana Tourism Board](https://banquet.haryanatourism.gov.in) *(Live Gov Project)*
> Redis-based distributed lock with timed checkout to prevent double-booking — BookMyShow's seat-hold pattern applied to venue management. Three separate frontends: admin, customer portal, agent panel.

**SpaEra** — Spa & Salon Management SaaS
> Full operations platform: appointments, staff, billing, PDF invoicing, QR workflows. Also wrote the business plan, segmented the market, designed the PLG flow, and presented pricing to the board.
